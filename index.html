About This App

PlasticWasteDetector is an AI-powered web application that helps identify waste materials using your device's camera. It can classify objects into 8 categories: Plastic, Metal, Glass, Paper, Wood, E-waste, Organic, and Other. This tool is designed to promote waste awareness and help with proper waste segregation for recycling and environmental conservation.
